# Regulation, Taxes, and Legal Basics

:::info Time estimate
**Reading:** 24 min · **Videos:** 3 min · **Exercise/review:** 10 min · **Total:** about 37 min
:::

## Learning objectives

By the end, you should be able to explain the core mechanism in plain language, identify its main risks, and apply it to a realistic decision.

## Lesson

### The core idea

Rules depend on jurisdiction, asset, activity, entity, and date. A token can raise securities, commodities, payments, sanctions, money-transmission, consumer-protection, and tax questions at the same time.

### How it works

In the United States, selling, exchanging, or spending digital assets can create taxable events; income may arise from compensation, mining, staking, airdrops, or other receipts depending on facts. Records should include date/time, asset, units, fair-market value, fees, basis, proceeds, wallet/exchange, and transaction identifier.

### Why it matters

Do not assume an exchange tax form captures transfers, cost basis, DeFi, or every wallet. Preserve raw exports and transaction hashes before services disappear. Consult a qualified professional for your facts.

### Safety-first takeaway

Legal status and agency guidance change. The course links to official regulators in Resources and deliberately avoids fixed tax-rate or asset-classification claims. Always check the current rule before acting.

## Knowledge check

<details>
<summary>What should you understand before risking funds?</summary>

The transaction path, custody model, fees, failure modes, recovery procedure, and legal/tax recordkeeping—not merely a price prediction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the safest response when you cannot explain a mechanism?</summary>

Pause. Research it with primary sources and, when appropriate, use a no-value simulation or small test rather than increasing exposure.

</details>
