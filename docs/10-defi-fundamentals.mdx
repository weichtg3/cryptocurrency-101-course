# DeFi Fundamentals

:::info Time estimate
**Reading:** 25 min · **Videos:** 28 min · **Exercise/review:** 10 min · **Total:** about 63 min
:::

## Learning objectives

By the end, you should be able to explain the core mechanism in plain language, identify its main risks, and apply it to a realistic decision.

## Lesson

### The core idea

DeFi uses smart contracts to exchange assets, lend, borrow, create derivatives, or coordinate liquidity. Permissionless access removes some gatekeepers but does not remove trust; trust shifts toward code, governance, oracles, collateral, bridges, and administrators.

### How it works

Automated market makers price assets from pool balances. Liquidity providers earn fees but hold a changing mix of assets. Impermanent loss describes underperformance versus simply holding when relative prices move; it can become permanent on withdrawal and fees may or may not offset it.

### Why it matters

Lending protocols require collateral and can liquidate a borrower when thresholds are crossed. Staking secures certain networks; “staking” offered by a company may instead be lending or a rewards program.

### Safety-first takeaway

High yield is compensation, subsidy, leverage, or risk—not free return. Inspect contract history, admin keys, oracle design, liquidity, audit limits, token incentives, and exit conditions. Audited does not mean safe.

3. Identify one claim you would verify with a primary source before acting.

## Knowledge check

<details>
<summary>What should you understand before risking funds?</summary>

The transaction path, custody model, fees, failure modes, recovery procedure, and legal/tax recordkeeping—not merely a price prediction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the safest response when you cannot explain a mechanism?</summary>

Pause. Research it with primary sources and, when appropriate, use a no-value simulation or small test rather than increasing exposure.

</details>
