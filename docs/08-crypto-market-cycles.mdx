# Crypto Market Cycles

:::info Time estimate
**Reading:** 18 min · **Videos:** 79 min · **Exercise/review:** 10 min · **Total:** about 107 min
:::

## Learning objectives

By the end, you should be able to explain the core mechanism in plain language, identify its main risks, and apply it to a realistic decision.

## Lesson

### The core idea

Crypto markets combine technology adoption, global liquidity, leverage, reflexive narratives, and thin trading in many assets. Rapid gains attract attention and leverage; falling collateral can force sales and accelerate declines.

### How it works

Bitcoin halvings are known supply events, not guaranteed price clocks. Macro conditions, regulation, failures, flows, and positioning can overwhelm any historical pattern. A small number of cycles is not a reliable statistical law.

### Why it matters

Volatility measures dispersion, not direction. Drawdown measures decline from a prior peak. Correlation often rises during stress, so a portfolio of many tokens may still behave like one concentrated bet.

### Safety-first takeaway

Write decisions before emotion arrives: maximum allocation, entry method, rebalance rule, loss capacity, custody plan, and conditions that invalidate the thesis. Never use money needed for bills, emergencies, taxes, or near-term goals.

## Knowledge check

<details>
<summary>What should you understand before risking funds?</summary>

The transaction path, custody model, fees, failure modes, recovery procedure, and legal/tax recordkeeping—not merely a price prediction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the safest response when you cannot explain a mechanism?</summary>

Pause. Research it with primary sources and, when appropriate, use a no-value simulation or small test rather than increasing exposure.

</details>
